>>[!multi-column]
>>>[!Info]
>>>>Vestibuloplasty is a surgical procedure performed by dental professionals to modify the shape and depth of the oral vestibule, which is the area between the gums and the inner surface of the lips or cheeks. This procedure is often recommended to address various dental and functional concerns, such as improving oral hygiene, enhancing denture fit and stability, or correcting certain aesthetic issues. -*[DrBestPrice](https://www.drbestprice.com/lexicon/vestibuloplasty)*
>>
>>>[!Info]
>>>>This is where the muscle is attached too close to the rim for the dentures to sit, therefore everytime the person moves their lips, their dentures move. The muscle is dissected off and re-sutured lower with a skin graft being placed on the rim. The graft can be taken from the hard palate or skin. -*MxFx FAD Book*
