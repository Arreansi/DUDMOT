## PiezoSurgery Handpiece Set Assembly Guide

>[!Assembly] PiezoSurgery Handpiece Assembly
>>[!Info] Assembly Guide
>>>[!multi-column] 
>>>>[!Blank]
>>>>![[PiezoSurgery Handpiece Assembly A.png]]
>>>
>>>>[!Blank|wide-3]
>>>>1. Install and screw the **Front Cone** to the Handpiece.
>>
>>>[!multi-column] 
>>>>[!Blank]
>>>>![[PiezoSurgery Handpiece Assembly B.png]]
>>>
>>>>[!Blank|wide-3]
>>>>2. Screw the chosen **PiezoSurgery Attachment** to the handpiece.
>>
>>>[!multi-column] 
>>>>[!Blank]
>>>>![[PiezoSurgery Handpiece Assembly C.png]]
>>>
>>>>[!Blank|wide-3]
>>>>3. Secure the attachment by first installing  the **K8 Mectron Torque Wrench** with its hollow part pointing towards the blade as indicated with the illustration provided
>>
>>>[!multi-column] 
>>>>[!Blank]
>>>>![[PiezoSurgery Handpiece Assembly D.png]]
>>>
>>>>[!Blank|wide-3]
