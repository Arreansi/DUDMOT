## Signature Drill Set Attachment Assembly Guide

>[!Assembly] Signature Drill Motor Assembly
>>[!Info] Signature Drill Motor to Machine
>>>[!multi-column] 
>>>>[!Blank] 
>>>>>![[Signature Drill Motor A.png|700]]
>>>>>Use with any of the three indicated [[Signature Drill Console]] motor ports.
>>>
>>>>[!Blank] 
>>>>>![[Signature Drill Motor B.png|550]]
>>>>>Align Red Circular Orientation Mark with the appropriate port before installing.
>


>[!Assembly] Signature Drill **5:1 Reducer - 8 cm** Attachment Assembly
>>[!Info] **Instructions as stated in the manual.**
>>>[!multi-column] 
>>>>[!Blank]
>>>>![[Signature Drill Red Attachment A.png]]
>>>
>>>>[!Blank|wide-3]
>>>>1. Install the attachment onto the handpiece until it securely snaps into place.
>>
>>>[!multi-column] 
>>>>[!Blank]
>>>>![[Signature Drill Red Attachment B.png]]
>>>
>>>>[!Blank|wide-3]
>>>>2. Rotate the lock collar to the **LOAD** position.
>>>>3. Insert the cutting accessory into the attachment until it bottoms.
>>
>>>[!multi-column] 
>>>>[!Blank]
>>>>![[Signature Drill Red Attachment C.png]]
>>>
>>>>[!Blank|wide-3]
>>>>4. Rotate the lock collar to the **RUN** position.
>>>>5. Gently tug on the cutting accessory to verify the accessory is locked in place and ready for use.
>>
>>**Removal:**
>>>[!multi-column] 
>>>>[!Blank]
>>>>![[Signature Drill Red Attachment D.png]]
>>>
>>>>[!Blank|wide-3]
>>>>1. Rotate the lock collar to the **LOAD** position.
>>>>2. Pull the cutting accessory straight out of the handpiece.
>

>[!Assembly]- Signature Drill **7cm Elite** Attachment Assembly
>>[!Info] **Instructions as stated in the manual.**
>>>[!multi-column] 
>>>>[!Blank]
>>>>![[Signature Drill Attachment A.png]]
>>>
>>>>[!Blank|wide-3]
>>>>1. While aligning the dots on the attachment and motor, slide the attachment onto the motor until it snaps into place.
>>
>>>[!multi-column] 
>>>>[!Blank]
>>>>![[Signature Drill Attachment B.png]]
>>>
>>>>[!Blank|wide-3]
>>>>2. Before inserting the cutting accessory, rotate the lock collar to the unlock position.
>>
>>>[!multi-column] 
>>>>[!Blank]
>>>>![[Signature Drill Attachment C.png]]
>>>
>>>>[!Blank|wide-3]
>>>>3. Carefully, insert the cutting accessory through the attachment tip.
>>
>>>[!multi-column] 
>>>>[!Blank]
>>>>![[Signature Drill Attachment D.png]]
>>>
>>>>[!Blank|wide-3]
>>>>4. Rotate the lock collar to the lock position to secure the cutting accessory in the attachment.
>>>>5. Gently tug both the attachment and the cutting accessory to verify they are secure.
>>
>>**Removal:**
>>>[!multi-column] 
>>>>[!Blank]
>>>>![[Signature Drill Attachment E.png]]
>>>
>>>>[!Blank|wide-3]
>>>>1. Unlock the collar to release the cutting accessory in the attachment.
>>>>2. Gently, remove the cutting accessory from the attachment tip.
>
