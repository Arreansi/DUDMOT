>>[!Info] Information
>>>They are used in Le Fort I, II, III procedures. After the separation of the maxilla from the pterygoid processes they are used alternatively on either side to rotate and advance the maxilla or midface, thus breaking or stretching the last resistances of the bone or scar tissue. A proper curvature of the thin blades  allow passing below the zygomatic bone.
